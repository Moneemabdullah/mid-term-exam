## Guiding principles

1. **Exam order first.** Start with the earliest exam, then protect time for later subjects.
2. **Credit weight matters.** The mid-term total is 30 marks per credit.
3. **Use actual syllabus size.** Do not estimate until the syllabus is available.
4. **Adjust for difficulty and confidence.** More time goes to harder or weaker areas.
5. **Interleave revision and practice.** Do not leave past questions until the end.
6. **Keep buffer time.** Leave space before each exam for final review and rest.

## Phase 5 — Weak topic review

**Goal:** Convert weak areas into reliable marks.

**Actions:**
- [ ] Review the subject `Weak Topics.md`.

## Phase 6 — Final preparation before each exam

**Goal:** Enter each exam calm and prepared.

**Actions:**
- [ ] Review the one-page recall sheet.

- [ ] Avoid new topics in the final session.

**Done when:** The exam-day sheet is complete and logistics are confirmed.

## Adaptable weekly rhythm

A day-by-day plan is in [Daily Study Lineup](Daily Study Lineup.md). Adjust the number and length of blocks to your actual availability.

- **Primary block:** the next exam subject.
- **Secondary block:** the following exam subject.
- **Revision block:** spaced review of earlier topics.
- **Practice block:** previous questions or a timed section.
- **Buffer:** one flexible session per week for overflow.

## Re-planning triggers

Revisit this plan when:

- A new syllabus or material set is added.
- A mock or practice result reveals a weak area.
- An exam time or venue changes.
- Your available study time changes.
- A subject is completed earlier or later than expected.
