# Learning Workflow

> [!tip] Use this with [Daily Study Lineup](/Daily Study Lineup.md)
> The lineup decides **what** to study each day. This page decides **how** to study each topic.

## The learning loop

| Step | Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Preview | 5 min | Read the topic heading, the subject's `Important Topics.md`, and the lecture summary outline. | A short list of what must be learned. |
| 2. First pass | 45 min | Read one topic in the subject's `Lecture Summary.md` with the matching slides open. | Definitions, formulas, and one example. |
| 3. Recall | 15 min | Close the notes and write the topic from memory. | A brain dump with gaps visible. |
| 4. Practice | 30 min | Solve a class-test question, numerical, or diagram for that topic. | A written answer, not a mental answer. |
| 5. Check | 10 min | Compare with the summary and source. Mark every error. | A short error list. |
| 6. Repair | 20 min | Re-study only the errors and missing links. | Corrected notes or flashcard. |
| 7. Review | 10 min | Re-test the same topic the next day, then after three days. | Evidence that it stayed learned. |

## Daily loop

1. Pick **one subject and one topic** from [Daily Study Lineup](/Daily Study Lineup.md).
2. Put the phone away and set a timer.
3. Use **50 minutes study + 10 minutes break**, or **25 + 5** if tired.
4. Start with recall from the previous session, not with rereading.
5. End the block with a five-line summary in your own words.
6. Mark a topic weak only when you have evidence: a wrong answer, a blank recall, or a formula you cannot reproduce.
7. Stop at the planned time. Sleep is part of the workflow.

## Subject order

1. **Artificial Intelligence** — earliest exam and highest marks.
2. **Data Mining** — Apriori numericals need repeated practice.
3. **Ethics** — definitions and canons need exact recall.
4. **Image Processing** — formulas and visual topics need diagrams.
5. **Sociology** — definitions and examples need structured answers.

## Exam-week rules

- Do not start a new topic after 8:00 PM the night before an exam.
- The last session is recall and past questions, not fresh reading.
- Write the one-page sheet from memory, then check it.
- Confirm exam time and venue before the final revision block.
- After each exam, rest before starting the next subject.

## Progress rule

Update [Progress Tracker](/Progress Tracker.md) only when one of these is true:

- You answered a previous question without notes.
- You reproduced a formula or diagram from memory.
- You explained a topic aloud without prompts.
- You corrected a weak topic and re-tested it.

Reading a summary is **not** completion.
