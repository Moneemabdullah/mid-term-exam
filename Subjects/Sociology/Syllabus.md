## Exam pattern

- Four questions, one from each part.
- The teacher suggestion lists **Part 3 Crime only**.
- CT-2 says “answer any two” from culture, corruption, and poverty.
- Corruption and poverty are **not** listed in the four-part suggestion; they are included here because they appear in CT-2 and are covered for completeness.

## Learning outcomes

You should be able to:

- Define sociology and explain its scope and relevance to engineering.
- Define culture and describe its main elements with examples.
- Explain the concept of crime and its social context.
- Describe social change, its causes, and its effects.
- Answer the CT-2 questions on culture, corruption, and poverty.
