## Learning outcomes

You should be able to:

- Define a digital image, pixel, resolution, and bit depth.
- Calculate storage requirements for grayscale and color images.
- Explain image acquisition, sampling, and quantization.
- Compare interpolation methods and explain aliasing.
- Describe human visual perception and its relevance to DIP.
- Apply negative, log, inverse-log, and power-law intensity transformations.
- Explain gamma correction and sRGB.
