## Learning outcomes

You should be able to:

- Define ethics, engineering ethics, and morals; distinguish ethics from morals, law, religion, and personal opinion.
- Explain why engineering ethics matters and describe its scope across the engineering lifecycle.
- Identify skills for ethical reasoning.
- Distinguish factual/descriptive, normative, and conceptual inquiries.
- Explain organizational and workplace ethics.
- Describe employee rights and employer/employee obligations.
- Apply the NSPE fundamental canons and rules of practice to a case.
- Answer the CT-1 and CT-2 questions on integrity, personal morals, organizational ethics, and responsibilities.
