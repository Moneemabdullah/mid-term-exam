## Learning outcomes

You should be able to:

- Explain data mining, the KDD process, and typical applications.
- Describe attribute types and basic statistical measures.
- Compute and interpret mean, median, mode, variance, covariance, and correlation.
- Measure similarity/dissimilarity for numeric, binary, nominal, and text data.
- Explain data quality problems and preprocessing tasks.
- Apply min-max, z-score, and decimal-scaling normalization.
- Explain and apply the Apriori algorithm, including support, confidence, lift, candidate generation, and pruning.
