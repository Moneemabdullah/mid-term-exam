## Learning outcomes

From the provided lecture notes, you should be able to:

- Define AI and describe its foundations, history, and applications.
- Define an agent, describe its structure, and classify agents and environments.
- Formulate a problem as a state-space search.
- Compare uninformed search strategies by completeness, optimality, time, and space.
- Explain A*, admissible heuristics, and consistent heuristics.
- Explain repeated-state handling and visited-node checking.
